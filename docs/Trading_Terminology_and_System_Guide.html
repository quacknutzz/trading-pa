EMA 10/30 Crossover

Exponential Moving Average (EMA) applies higher mathematical weight to recent candles. When the fast 10-period EMA crosses above the slow 30-period EMA on a 10-minute candle, it flags that immediate short-term momentum has decoupled positively from the session average.

Strategy Use: Master Entry Trigger (Bullish = Buy, Bearish = Sell).
Watch Out: Entering on forming candles triggers false whipsaws before the candle closes.

Pullback Limit Order

Instead of buying at current market price (LTP) and chasing expensive breakouts, the engine calculates the nearest 5m EMA (10, 30, 50, 100, 200) below the price for BUY orders, placing a resting limit order at that dynamic support level.

Why It's Smart: Ensures you "Buy the Dip" at technical support rather than getting trapped at the candle high.
Expiry Rule: If unfilled within 3 completed 5m bars (15 mins), order is cancelled.

RSI (Relative Strength Index)

Oscillator ranging from 0 to 100 measuring price speed and velocity magnitude. In momentum trend systems, the 52–64 range confirms clean buying power without the exhaustion risk that occurs above 75.

Strategy Use: Filters out weak momentum or over-extended trades.

ADX (Average Directional Index)

Measures the strength of a trend regardless of direction. ADX < 20 indicates a choppy, sideways consolidation where trend bots get chopped to pieces. ADX > 20 confirms active directional flow.

Entry Gate: Entry requires Stock ADX ≥ 20 (avoids losing money in chop).

ATR (Average True Range)

Calculates the true average price movement of a stock per candle in absolute rupees. A high-beta defense stock moves ₹15 per 5m bar, while large-cap banks move ₹4.

Why It Matters: Fixed percentage stops fail because volatile stocks breach 1% in 2 minutes of normal market noise.
Best Practice: Set stops to 1.5x ATR instead of a naive flat percentage.

Slippage & Bid-Ask Spread

The difference between the expected signal price and the actual traded price. Market orders fill at the opposing Bid or Ask. During volatility, spread slippage extracts 0.04% to 0.10% per round trip.

Root Cause: Latency, order book depth gaps, and queue position priority.
V6 Defense: Placing passive limit orders at EMA support saves spread cost.

MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion) is the highest profit a trade reached before close. MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion) is the maximum drawdown suffered during the trade.
