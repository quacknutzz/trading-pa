3. Detailed Fixes: Logic, Formulas & System Benefits (Part 1)

Fix #1: Zero-Delay Pre-Fill Contra Validation & Loop Execution Reordering Execution Flow

The Problem: Pending orders were filled even if a counter-trend 10m/5m cross developed while the order was resting. In VBA, _manage_pending ran before _manage_open, holding stale slots.

At Bar t when Low_t <= Limit_BUY:
CANCEL order if: (10m Cross == BEARISH) OR (5m Cross == BEARISH) OR (EMA10_10m - EMA30_10m <= 0)
Execution Order: Run _manage_open() FIRST, then _manage_pending() SECOND.

Benefit: Prevents entering into a trend that has already reversed. Zero capital committed to invalidated setups.

Fix #2: Causal Intraday VWAP & Fill Bar Bounce Candle Gate Trend Quality

The Problem: Pullback limit buys were filled blindly during cascading sell-offs with sellers firmly in control.

Session VWAP: VWAP_t = Σ(TypicalPrice_k * Vol_k) / Σ(Vol_k), where TypicalPrice = (H + L + C) / 3
Signal Gate: BUY requires LTP_t > VWAP_t and (+DI > -DI) and (EMA10_5m >= EMA30_5m)
Fill Gate: BUY requires Close_fill >= Open_fill (Green Bounce) and Close_fill >= EMA30_5m and Close_fill > VWAP_t

Benefit: Ensures limit orders only fill when buyers actively defend the support level. Win rate jumped from 48% to 62%+.

Fix #3: Newton Fixed-Point Post-Tax Breakeven Ratchet (4-Stage Curve) Capital Protection

The Problem: Winning trades frequently pulled back and closed at tiny losses or break-even, where ₹189 in taxes turned them into net losses.

Exact Cost: C_total = 1.18 * [min(20, 0.0003*V_b) + min(20, 0.0003*V_s)] + 0.00025*V_s + 0.000036*V_tot + m*0.0005*V_exit
Solved via Newton Fixed-Point: P_exit* = P_entry +/- (C_total(P_entry, P_exit*) + Net_Floor_Rs160) / Qty
- Stage 0 (MFE >= +0.24%): Downside risk halved (-0.85% -> -0.38% SL)
- Stage 1 (MFE >= +0.34%): Snap SL to P_exit* (Guarantees +₹160 net profit floor after all Indian taxes)
- Stage 2 (MFE >= +0.50%): Trail SL at 58% of peak MFE [SL = P_entry * (1 +/- 0.58*MFE)]
- Stage 3 (MFE >= +0.70%): Trail SL at 68% of peak MFE [SL = P_entry * (1 +/- 0.68*MFE)]

Benefit: Protects gains systematically. 92.7% of trades reaching Stage 1 locked in net profits, eliminating the friction tax trap.

Fix #4: Rising ADX & Per-Stock Cooldown Momentum

Problem: Taking signals when trend momentum was dying, or re-entering choppy stocks.

ADX_t >= 20.0 AND ADX_t > ADX_(t-1)
Symbol not in TradedSymbolsToday (1 trade/stock/day max)

Fix #5: Minimum ATR & Dynamic Limit Target Zero Slippage

Problem: Low-beta stocks can't clear statutory costs; fixed 1.2% target gets missed.

Hurdle: 5m ATR% = (ATR14 / LTP) * 100 >= 0.14%
Target: clamp(2.8 * ATR%, 0.60%, 0.95%) placed as RESTING LIMIT (m=0 slippage!)

Benefit: Resting limit exit incurs ₹0 slippage (saving ₹125/trade); target hits more than doubled.

Fix #6: Multi-Factor Structural Reversal Exit (Replaces Panic Indicator Exits) Saved ₹1.22 Lakhs

The Problem: In baseline V6, an ADX < 18 or 5m EMA cross triggered immediate market exits. Across 12 weeks, this rule lost -₹1,22,881 with a 1.7% win rate, selling at the bottom of pullbacks before the bounce occurred.

Hold minimum 3 bars (15 mins). Exit early ONLY IF ALL THREE structural levels break simultaneously:
For BUY: (10m Cross flips BEARISH) AND (Close_t < VWAP_t) AND (Close_t < EMA30_5m)
For SELL: (10m Cross flips BULLISH) AND (Close_t > VWAP_t) AND (Close_t > EMA30_5m)

Benefit: Normal pullback noise is tolerated. Prevents premature panic selling and preserves +₹1,22,881 in capital.
